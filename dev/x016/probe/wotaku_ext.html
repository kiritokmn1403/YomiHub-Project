How-to ​

Adding store ​

  • Auto: Click on Install and there will be a pop-up window in the app. Click Add.
  • Manual:
    • Copy the URL.
    • Then go to Browse -> Extensions -> -> Extension stores.
    • Click Add, paste the URL there and click Add

Adding Extension ​

  1. After adding the store, go to Home -> Browse -> Extensions.
  2. Install your extension by clicking .
  3. You will now see it listed in Sources.
